07 / TRANSACTION INTEGRITY

A paid entry is a verified state,
not just a thank-you page.

Validated entry→PaymentIntent→Stripe event→Paid order→Eligibility review
The current code creates Stripe PaymentIntents and has signed webhook verification for successful events. For CRO Trail, successful payment creates an entry pending eligibility review; a valid ticket must follow organiser approval. The live release must verify asynchronous and failed payment cases, refunds, duplicate events, operational reconciliation, document review, ticket delivery and support workflows with real credentials and a public endpoint.

the race rules.

Athletes choose their affiliation and enter its membership number.

Certificate upload, expiry date and athlete attestation are part of the entry form.

03 / NON-MEMBERS+ €10 in the demo

A configurable supplement is included in the total before payment.

04 / ORGANISER REVIEWPayment ≠ approval

The paid entry stays pending until its eligibility is checked; no valid ticket is issued yet.

The €10 supplement is a demo value pending CRO Trail's final rules. Certificate files are stored privately; the live launch also requires an approved medical-data notice and an organiser review process.

The current prototype now collects the affiliation or non-member choice, membership number where relevant, athletics medical certificate, expiry date and attestation in Italian and English. The backend calculates the non-member surcharge, currently €10 in test mode. A successful payment leaves the entry pending review and suppresses valid ticket download. Before production, confirm the final eligibility rules, amount, data notice, event-date certificate validity and who performs document approval.
